Scope

- Create a lively, controllable 3D avatar for Medlar that runs entirely local, reacts to text/audio, and needs no button presses.
- Use free tools for asset creation if desired; runtime remains service‑free and offline.
Core Behaviors

- Idle presence: breathing hover, subtle wing flaps, tail flicks, random blinks, micro head tilts.
- Emotions: neutral , happy , curious , angry , sad , surprised , smug .
- Speech reaction: mouth shapes (visemes), jaw bob, eye focus and brow motion synced to phonemes or amplitude.
- Movement: fly, glide, land, orbit a target, move to coordinates, follow a path, look at a point, follow the camera.
- Gestures: wave wing, point with wing, tail wag, wink, tiny bow, hands‑off “thinking” pose.
- Attention cues: glow pulse, wing flare, horn twitch when events occur (mentions, alerts).
Modes

- idle : ambient loops with occasional micro‑gestures.
- chat : prioritize lip‑sync and eye contact; gentle hover; expressive reactions.
- present : slower, confident motions; gesture emphasis.
- alert : sharper motions, focused gaze, tail stiffening.
- sleep : low energy, reduced motion, soft breathing.
Autonomy Rules

- Sentiment mapping: positive → happy or curious ; negative → sad or angry ; surprised keywords → surprised .
- Context triggers: “attention/important” → alert ; “joke/fun” → smug/happy .
- Activity cadence: rotate minor gestures every 20–40 seconds; avoid repetition; respect mode .
- Safety: never spam rapid motions; enforce min durations and cooldowns.
Movement Set

- move_to : position with ease‑in/out.
- set_velocity : continuous drift; safe speed caps.
- look_at : aim head/eyes at a target; smooth tracking.
- orbit : radius, speed, plane; suitable for “present” mode.
- land / takeoff : transition sequences; wings adjust posture.
Gesture Set

- wing_wave , wing_point_left/right
- tail_flick_short/long
- wink_left/right
- bow_small
- flare_wings
- horn_twitch
Each gesture has duration, intensity, and interrupt rules (e.g., speech can blend over gestures, but alert may preempt).

Voice & Lip Sync

- Input: text or audio file.
- Offline TTS (optional): Piper/Coqui; store audio locally.
- Lip‑sync: viseme timing via Rhubarb Lip Sync or amplitude‑based fallback.
- Viseme map: AI , E , O , U , FV , L , MBP , WQ → shape keys; smooth interpolation; clamp extremes.
Visual FX

- Eye highlights and blinks; occasional squint for emotion emphasis.
- Glow ring or rim‑light pulse for reactions; color shifts per mode.
- Shadow and light positioning to keep silhouette readable.
I/O Protocol (Local, Programmatic)

- Commands accepted as local messages (e.g., JSON over WebSocket or HTTP ). No external services required.
- Categories:
  - emote : set emotion
  - say : speak text or play audio with viseme timings
  - mode : change behavior mode
  - move_to / set_velocity / look_at / orbit / land / takeoff
  - gesture : play named gesture
  - fx : glow/color pulse
  - idle : enable/disable ambient motions
- Responses: ack , error , state snapshot, completed events.
Animation Layering

- Base: locomotion (hover/fly/land).
- Overlay: gestures (wing/tail/wink).
- Face: brows/eyes/mouth visemes/emotions.
- FX: glow pulses/color shifts.
Blend priority: face > gestures > base; ensure clean transitions and no hard pops.

Performance Targets

- 60 FPS on a mid‑range Windows laptop.
- Draw calls and polycount kept modest; animations blended on GPU/CPU efficiently.
- Entire runtime and assets loaded from local disk; zero network calls.
Free Tooling (Optional for Asset Creation)

- Blender (rig + shape keys + animations).
- Rhubarb Lip Sync (visemes).
- Piper/Coqui (offline TTS).
- Mixamo clips (free) if you want extra gestures; retarget in Blender.
- All exported as GLB/GLTF and stored locally; runtime uses local files only.
Asset Convention

- Model: medlar.glb with armature Armature , bones Wing.L , Wing.R , Tail , Head .
- Shape keys: Viseme_AI , Viseme_E , Viseme_O , Viseme_U , Viseme_FV , Viseme_L , Viseme_MBP , Viseme_WQ , plus emotions Emote_Happy , Emote_Sad , Emote_Angry , Emote_Surprised , Emote_Smug , Emote_Curious .
- Animations: Idle_Hover , Fly_Loop , Land , Takeoff , Wing_Wave , Tail_Flick , Wink_Left/Right .
Security & Privacy

- All assets and libraries stored locally.
- No external accounts; no data leaves the machine.
- Simple access control (local only) to prevent outside control.
